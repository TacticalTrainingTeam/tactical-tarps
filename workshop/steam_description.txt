[h1]Tactical Tarps[/h1]
A framework for laying out a tarp for various use cases. Deploy an item from your inventory into a fully-fledged field station, then pack it up again when you're done - no trace left behind.

Comes with 5 ready-to-use tarps:

[list]
[*] [b]Signal Tarp[/b] - Large, colored tarp for marking your position and signalling helicopters from a distance.
[*] [b]Medic Tarp[/b] - Turns into an ACE Medical Facility on the fly, ideal for casualty collection points.
[*] [b]Drone Tarp[/b] - Spawns an invisible helipad so drones can land exactly where you want them to.
[*] [b]Hazmat Tarp[/b] - A drop-off point for contaminated items - place your gear on it and it's gone.
[*] [b]Repair Tarp[/b] - Black tarp that keeps your clothes clean while working on vehicles, and provides an ACE Repair Facility while deployed.
[/list]

[h1]Usage[/h1]
[list]
[*] Have one of the mod's items in your inventory.
[*] Lay it out via the ACE Self-Interaction menu.
[*] Pick it back up via ACE Interaction.
[*] Tweak spawned props and other details via CBA Settings.
[/list]

Most tarps come in multiple colors, and the framework is built to be extended with new tarp types.
